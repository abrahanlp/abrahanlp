# Hi, I'm Abraham Lemos :wave:

Welcome to this place! The main purpose opening a _GitHub_ profile is to share some of my personal projects and technical challenges with you. Does _'some'_ means there are more? Absolutely, those with other people involved or proprietary work can't be public easly. On the other hand, my full-time job consumes most of my productive time, buy I will always make space to make progress here. Moreover, this is a place where you can learn a bit more about my technical skills and experience background.

Like many of you, my journey in _tech_ started as a kid, back when _"tech"_ just meant taking apart everything I could get my hands on. As you grow older, the things you disassemble become both more expensive (radios, TVs, motorcycles, cars, my mom's oven...) and more complex. Over time, disciplines like electricity, mechanics and electronics blend together, and we think that them will be with you forever. Eventually, the _Education System_ forces you to choose a path, and **_electronics_** was mine.

Driven by deep curiosity and passion, I’ve faced more problems than I could possibly list. My professional career started as _**Electronic Technician**_, where I gained valuable hands-on experience in manufacturing, assembly, testing, diagnosis, maintenance and reverse engineering of complex electronic systems. This practical background became one of my greatest assets when I later earned my degree in **_Electronic Engineering_** while working full-time, a challenge that truly forged my organization, dedication, and perseverance.
